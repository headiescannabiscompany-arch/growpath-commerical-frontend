# GrowPathAI Hat Owner-Source Register

Locked: 2026-08-27

This register prevents supplier imagery, generated review sheets or later guesses from
silently changing the owner's hat directions. The files below are the controlling source
roster for translating the selected Triple Bag-style hats into GrowPathAI hats.

## Conversion contract

1. Only a registered owner source may originate a design direction. Adding another source
   requires explicit owner approval and its filename and SHA-256 must be added here first.
2. Preserve the selected source's crown/brim color blocking, silhouette, decoration layout,
   relative emblem scale and overall visual energy.
3. Replace only Triple Bag Genetics and third-party identity. Use an existing approved
   GrowPathAI mark from `source-art/` without redrawing, ornamenting, enclosing or restyling it.
4. Do not invent shields, triangles, bags, wings, flames, fronds, bursts, badges, team marks,
   manufacturer marks, stickers, collaborations or slogans.
5. Supplier references may establish a real blank's model, textile, construction, factory
   color and availability. They may not originate a design or override a registered source.
6. If the owner source cannot be matched by a stock supplier option, preserve the source and
   label the result as a custom blank/color-blocking direction pending written feasibility,
   cost, minimum, lead-time and sample approval. Do not substitute a different stock design.
7. An individually owner-approved concept image supersedes aggregate sheets and source mockups.
   Approved concepts are not reopened unless the owner explicitly requests a revision.
8. A concept rendering is not a stitch file or manufactured sample. `DST`, editable native
   embroidery source and an exact-blank physical sew-out remain separate production gates.
