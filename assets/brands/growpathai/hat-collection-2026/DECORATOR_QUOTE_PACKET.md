## Quote request scope

Please quote a pre-production sample for the selected GrowPathAI concepts on owner-supplied
BLVNK blanks. Start with one design from each construction family before digitizing all 16:

1. GP-01 Circuit Crimson on BLV600 Maroon wool blend.
2. GP-09 Sage Circuit Rope on BLV550 Sage/Olive nylon.
3. GP-13 Grow Together Cord on BLV551 Black/Black irregular-wale corduroy.
4. GP-15 Blue Signal Cord on BLV603 Air Blue micro corduroy.

For each sample, quote blank handling, digitizing, each placement, backing/topping, thread,
sample sew-out, revisions, shipping/tax, decorated quantity tiers and lead time. State whether
the shop can source BLVNK directly or requires owner-supplied blanks.

## Required answers

- Will the customer receive the final `DST` and the editable native source (`EMB`, `PXF`,
  `OFM`, or equivalent) with unrestricted reuse for GrowPathAI production?
- What machine, cap driver/hoop, needle, backing, topping and thread brand will be used?
- Will the front sequence be planned for the curved cap and center seam rather than reused
  from a flat-garment file?
- Can the shop preserve 1.2 mm circuit traces and 2.2 mm open nodes at the target size? If not,
  what redraw is required?
- Can the shop produce a photographed actual sew-out on each exact fabric before approval?
- Can it supply physical thread cards or named thread IDs for lime, blue, forest, gold,
  burgundy, red, violet, stone, white and black?
- How many revisions are included, and who owns every revision and source file?
- What are setup/digitizing/sample costs, minimums, unit tiers, packaging, shipping and lead
  times? Do later color-only changes require a new digitizing fee?
- Can it embroider the side and small rear placements without disassembling or distorting the
  cap? What exact rear minimum text height is reliable?
- Can it produce both direct embroidery and woven/embroidered patches for GP-13/GP-14?

## Selection rule

Select only a provider that answers the file-ownership, exact-blank sew-out, machine/setup and
revision questions in writing. A low quote without editable file return or a physical sew-out
does not satisfy R-05.
