# Cannabis Visibility Policy

Show cannabis-specific content only when grow context, interests or account purpose includes cannabis/hemp. Do not expose it by default to unrelated horticulture viewers.

Cannabis-specific AI shortcuts must use structured crop types, grow interests/tags, explicit cannabis flags, or a reviewed/user-confirmed plant identity. Free-text notes, exclusions, negative statements such as "not cannabis," and rejected or unconfirmed AI candidates do not establish cannabis context.

Cannabis-specific surfaces include flower-day/flip language, trichome harvest review, cultivar/genetics and mother/clone selection, cannabis pheno categories, flower dry/cure, cannabis presets and cannabis facility examples.

General users receive crop-neutral identification, plant care, watering, soil/fertility, IPM, logs, courses and community language. A crop-neutral question about visible plant sex or reproductive stage is general botany guidance and must not unlock cannabis-only tools or wording. Visibility controls presentation and discovery; it must not weaken data access control. Private cannabis media and records remain scoped to their owner/workspace.

Public regulated-cannabis discovery is an explicit cannabis-commerce context. Do not include regulated cannabis products in default horticulture storefront results; surface them only after the visitor deliberately chooses regulated discovery or follows a direct lawful-business link. A business may hold more than one reviewed role, including nursery, cultivator, breeder or seed bank, distributor, dispensary, and retailer. Do not infer transaction authority from its name, plan, or role alone.

A published regulated business may show its profile, verified public website, and informational inventory when those records satisfy publication and cannabis-visibility rules. Transaction capabilities are separate, progressively granted permissions: catalog display, external product handoff, inquiry, reservation, GrowPath checkout, payment, pickup, delivery, domestic shipping, export, and import. Grant each capability only for a versioned, reviewed seller + business authorization + product class + origin + destination + buyer eligibility + fulfillment route. An applicable license or authorization must be current where required. Unknown, expired, conflicting, or unsupported evidence defaults to informational inventory with the transaction action withheld. A warning, seller attestation, or age checkbox does not make an unlawful transaction lawful.

Policy changes such as United States federal legalization do not automatically enable every cannabis transaction. Update the versioned jurisdiction policy and re-evaluate the complete route, including state, Tribal, territorial, local, destination, carrier, payment-provider, tax, marketplace, and app-store constraints that still apply. Lawful international seed or cannabis routes may be enabled only when both origin/export and destination/import requirements and the exact product classification are supported. Entering regulated discovery must not unlock unrelated cannabis grow tools.

Crop identification is the evidence-handling exception, not a discovery exception. When a user deliberately submits a clear plant photo, the general crop-identification workflow may identify visible cannabis or hemp morphology even without an attached grow. The result remains an unconfirmed crop-level draft, must not infer cultivar/strain from appearance, and must not unlock or advertise unrelated cannabis-only tools. Cannabis-specific planning, genetics, harvest, dry/cure, clone-selection, and facility surfaces remain gated by structured grow context, account interests, or an eligible account purpose.
