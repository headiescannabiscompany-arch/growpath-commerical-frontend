# Harvest, Dry and Cure

Harvest readiness combines breeder timing, flower day, usable trichome evidence from photographed areas, pistils, swell, aroma trend, broader plant maturity and effect goal. No photo set produces a literal whole-plant clear/cloudy/amber percentage: every visual percentage is an estimate of the intact heads visible in the inspected sampled areas. Standard phone photos may support a wider, lower-confidence sampled-area estimate when enough heads are resolved. Request a targeted replacement only when the visible sampled heads themselves cannot support a defensible estimate.

The product and workflow name is **Harvest Readiness**. Trichome photo review is one evidence component and must not be presented as the name or full scope of the tool. User-facing summaries must describe the multi-signal review rather than reducing readiness to a trichome estimator.

The photo workflow must show its acquisition checklist before analysis: at least three sharp macro samples from top, middle, and lower bud sites; intact gland heads on bud calyxes in focus; neutral white light without blur, glare, purple LEDs, digital zoom, or heavy compression; and a wider bud-context photo for location. Upload errors, unavailable analysis, incomplete provider responses, and unusable photos must fill no trichome percentages and must repeat actionable retake guidance.

The minimum provider-ready set is therefore four uploaded photos: three macro bud-site samples plus one wider context photo. Block analysis before provider use when the set is incomplete, state how many photos remain, and use no AI credit. A standard completed review of four through twelve images costs one AI credit whether the set proves usable or needs better photos. Thirteen through eighty images is an explicit **Deep Review** whose quoted cost is `ceil(image count / 12)` credits, up to seven credits at eighty images. Show the exact image count and cost and require acceptance before dispatch. Deep Review is available to an eligible Personal/free account that has enough AI credits; it must not add a separate paid-plan entitlement gate. Commercial and Facility workspace authorization and billing boundaries still apply. Reserve the full quoted amount once, only after workspace authority and private, AI-approved evidence are verified. A failure durably proven to precede every provider dispatch refunds the full reservation. Once any batch reaches its durable dispatch boundary or an earlier batch has completed, publish no partial review, never send the batch again, and keep the accepted reservation for support reconciliation when a safe final result cannot be committed.

A Standard review uses one deterministic durable identity for the exact authenticated workspace, optional grow and plant, selected evidence order and original-byte digests, sanitized provider context, model, image detail, prompt, review policy, and source-video selection binding. An exact completed replay must return the stored normalized result without another provider call or credit charge. Only a failure durably proven to have occurred before the provider-dispatch claim may be refunded automatically; that guarded refund releases the unused request identity so the same exact evidence can begin a fresh charged attempt after the pre-dispatch problem is corrected. Once that claim starts, the same exact request must never be dispatched again or automatically refunded: replay a securely completed stored result when available, otherwise expose an unresolved reconciliation state without telling the user to retry. Deep Review applies the same dispatch boundary independently to each durable batch and also treats any earlier completed batch as provider work that cannot be automatically refunded. While an exact Deep evidence set for the same actor and workspace remains failed with credits reserved for reconciliation, a different client-operation key must not bypass that hold or create another reservation or provider dispatch.

Harvest Readiness accepts up to 12 direct user photos so uneven canopies can include additional representative top, middle, lower, and wider context views. A private video may contribute a server-selected retained frame set, but direct photos plus retained frames may never exceed 80 provider-ready images. Four remains the minimum provider-ready set; both 12 direct photos and 80 combined images are ceilings, not targets. Photo count is not role coverage: even 80 usable wide or mid-range views remain insufficient when three true macros do not resolve individual intact gland heads at top, middle, and lower bud sites. Reject obviously tiny or invalid files before upload. Resolution metadata can warn about likely loss of macro detail, but it cannot establish intact gland-head focus, neutral light, glare, sample location, or representative coverage. The image review must report those limitations and request the exact replacement view.

Send qualified photo evidence to the vision provider at original image detail by default when the dedicated configured Harvest model supports it; fall back to high detail only for an incompatible model or an explicit operational override. The review must return set-level focus, glare, lighting, gland-head visibility and role-coverage checks plus one finding for each supplied image. Each finding records the claimed or visually supported role, every named non-overlapping trichome-rich region used for the tally, focus, glare, visible-head detail, whether the image can support the distribution and why it was excluded. Digital enlargement can expose existing pixels but cannot recreate detail erased by blur, compression or clipped highlights. Exclude glare-obscured heads; localized glare is acceptable only when separate glare-free, sharp gland heads remain. A model result cannot override these server-side role and quality guards.

Harvest uses a dedicated current full-size vision model rather than inheriting a cost-optimized global mini model. Keep the model configurable through `OPENAI_HARVEST_VISION_MODEL`, record the exact provider model in the signed review, and validate model changes against representative rights-cleared harvest evidence before calling the change verified. A stronger model may improve fine-detail interpretation, but it does not relax the focus, glare, role-coverage, percentage, receipt, or user-confirmation safeguards.

Treat trichome counting as a separately calibrated vision task rather than assuming that a general image prompt is a validated counter. The rights-reviewed seed catalog may contain qualitative clear/cloudy/amber panels and annotated count-method references, but it must state which cases lack per-head labels and which annotations leak the answer. The staging qualitative seed must span more than one independent primary source, retain reviewed tight crops and cryptographic source pins where packaged supplements are used, and remain explicitly separate from quantitative counter calibration. Do not report a production-ready quantitative counter from collages, one owner case, or a single genotype. Acceptance requires diverse ordinary-phone and macro captures, neutral and difficult lighting, resolved head-level labels or independently reviewed counts, explicit glare and warm-cast boundaries, and staging-only comparison against the currently deployed model. Score head detection separately from maturity classification on the same blinded set. Because the deployed aggregate counter does not return per-head boxes, compare its exact per-image tallies without inventing detector locations; hold any detector/classifier replacement to independent absolute detection, resolved-class, amber, false-amber, completeness and uncertainty-interval floors in addition to non-regression against the deployed aggregate amber range. Use the published detector-plus-classifier and color-space methods as architecture evidence, not as permission to copy unavailable datasets or claim their reported accuracy in GrowPath.

The minimum four-photo Harvest set must receive an overlapping full-area macro grid for every original, including the wider or brightest final image. The grid must reach the left, right, top, and bottom bounds of an ordinary portrait, landscape, or square photo instead of inspecting only a centered column or the sharpest-looking region. Overlap prevents gaps but requires positional deduplication before counts are aggregated. Larger sets may bound derived views for provider reliability, but every untouched original remains present at the highest supported detail. Never silently omit a spatial region from one member of the minimum set and then characterize that photo as fully zoom-inspected.

Provider-bound Standard originals must come only from authorized server-loaded private bytes, never from a URL for the provider to fetch. Normalize them sequentially into metadata-free rendered copies and enforce a 48 MiB total after normalization as well as the existing selected-original limit; expansion past that boundary fails before dispatch with actionable re-export guidance. The exact four-photo minimum requires its complete bounded full-area crop plan and fails before dispatch if that plan cannot fit. For five through twelve originals, retain every untouched original and add only bounded supplemental crops in round-robin source order; reaching the 24-view or 48 MiB derived-crop ceiling stops supplemental crop generation without blocking the original set or claiming omitted crops were inspected.

The signed review-policy and prompt versions must change when the sampling geometry or classification instructions materially change. Do not compare an older centered-crop review with a full-area-grid review as repeatability evidence from one method. The client may reopen older signed reviews, but new calculations and calibration feedback must retain the exact policy version that produced the estimate.

Client preflight may reject an obviously tiny image and warn when the short side is below the shared 900-pixel diagnostic preference, but pixel dimensions alone cannot decide whether intact gland heads are resolved. In particular, a normal 1080-by-1920 phone photo must not be labeled unresolvable solely because its short side is below 1200 pixels. Subject scale, focus, compression, glare, lighting, calyx placement, and resolved-head detail belong to the server image review using the original and source-bound diagnostic crops. When saved evidence is restored, a retired dimension-only warning must be discarded at the read boundary while every still-current focus, glare, compression, lighting, and provenance finding remains visible.

Every AI image workflow must preserve and inspect the untouched original-resolution image and may add server-created diagnostic crops from the original pixels. Harvest uses systematic coverage crops; other workflows may use center and attention-selected crops. These enlarged views supplement the original instead of replacing or downscaling it. They remain bound to their source image and never count as an additional photo, sample site, observation, role, or independent evidence. The staging trichome counter may also derive one bounded luminance-audit view of a detected candidate by applying the same recorded multiplier to every RGB channel. That view may expose structure or opacity hidden by a dark capture, but it preserves no new pixels, is not independent evidence, cannot restore blur or clipping, and cannot confirm amber when the original cannot separate head-contained color from lighting or tissue. Crop or audit creation failure must fall back to the untouched original rather than rejecting the upload.
Every successful Harvest review must retain a digest-bound manifest of the exact systematic
coverage crops inspected. The immediate and saved results let an authorized user view and save
an individual crop or export the complete viewer-friendly crop set with source-photo number,
strategy, source rectangle, output dimensions, review provenance, and the inspected-area-only
limitation. Regenerate a view only from its retained original and serve it only when its digest
matches; never silently substitute a newly chosen crop or imply that a crop is another sample.

When a large source photo produces more detector tiles than the staging trichome counter can inspect within its bounded request, tile selection must preserve spatial coverage of the photographed area before filling remaining slots by sharpness. Global sharpness ranking alone must not concentrate the sample in one bright or textured area and silently omit the rest of the photographed bud. This improves use of the supplied image; it does not turn tiles into independent sites or make an unrepresentative photo representative of the plant.

Harvest image review records amber visibility separately from a complete trichome distribution. When intact amber-colored gland heads are visible but the full clear/cloudy/amber distribution cannot be calculated, preserve the bounded amber observation and its exact image/region basis while leaving percentages blank. Do not turn that bounded observation into a harvest date. When representative top, middle, and lower calyx samples support an advanced amber share, treat the harvest window as open for immediate review; fresh pistils may reflect uneven, shaded, foxtailing, or newly developing tissue and cannot by themselves force a delay. Breeder timing remains context rather than a mandatory finish date.

Assess gland-head development separately from color. Record whether resolved heads appear predominantly developing, intact/turgid and visibly swollen, mixed, or show advanced senescence, and retain the exact image/region basis plus only the visible signals: small developing heads, intact turgid heads, visibly swollen heads, wrinkling, collapse, resin exudation, fusion, ruptured heads, bare stalks, or detached/missing heads. Advanced senescence requires resolved repeated morphology rather than one blur, clipped highlight, damaged hair, or compression artifact. Without a scale or a same-plant sequence, do not infer absolute head size or treat swelling as proof of chemistry, potency, or a harvest date. Color alone does not establish oxidation, chemistry, rupture, bare-stalk formation, or gland-head death. Head development is supporting evidence that must remain separate from clear/cloudy/amber tallies and be interpreted with representative site coverage, calyx swelling, pistils, timing, and aroma context.

Report visible-sample evidence whenever enough intact gland heads can be distinguished in any inspected calyx regions, even when site coverage, context, lighting, or focus is not strong enough for calculator autofill. Tally each clearly resolved intact head once across all named non-overlapping calyx regions in each original as clear, cloudy, confirmed amber, amber-or-warm-light, or cloudy-or-glare. Never count bright pixels, sparkles, pistils, plant hairs, colored plant tissue, blurred circles, compression artifacts, sugar-leaf edge heads, or the same head repeated in overlapping crops. Clear requires a resolved transparent head; cloudy requires diffuse interior opacity rather than a small glowing reflection; confirmed amber requires yellow/orange/brown color inside the resolved head that can be separated from warm light, a pistil, the background, or purple/red plant tissue. Put a resolved head that itself appears yellow/orange/tan/brown but cannot be separated from a warm cast or nearby colored tissue in amber-or-warm-light. Put genuinely ambiguous resolved white heads in cloudy-or-glare. Under mixed or warm light, compare the head interior with immediately adjacent highlights, clear heads, and transmitted background, then audit every head assigned clear: a visibly colored resolved head cannot remain clear merely because the entire image is warm. Unresolved colored cases move to amber-or-warm-light, not confirmed amber. Colored amber uncertainty must never be hidden in the white/glare bucket or forced into confirmed amber. The server sums eligible per-photo tallies and calculates the five-part visible-sample percentage, records how many heads were counted, and labels counting confidence as low below 20 heads, medium from 20-49, and high from 50 upward. Display directly confirmed amber as a strict evidence floor and confirmed plus amber-or-warm-light as the possible amber total, with the ambiguity count and lighting limitation visible. State that the directly confirmed floor is not a claim that the photographed sample is probably that low. Five countable heads may support coarse low-confidence visible-sample evidence; it does not support whole-plant inference. Label the exact image regions and state plainly that the percentages describe only visible sampled heads, never the entire plant. Preserve and display the five-bucket counts, percentage components, region, resolved-head total, and counting confidence for every eligible original photo separately in both the immediate review and saved result. Call out the photo area with the strongest confirmed-to-possible amber signal so a locally advanced area cannot disappear inside the aggregate, while explicitly requiring comparison with the other sampled areas and prohibiting whole-plant inference from that photo. Representative top/middle/lower coverage remains a separate confidence and calculator-autofill question; it must not erase defensible sampled-area evidence. A saved Personal Harvest run must reopen its exact retained evidence with the canonical grow context through one explicit retry action; loading the photos does not spend a credit or start analysis.

After a signed Harvest image review, let the authenticated user record whether the visible-area estimate looks close, whether amber appears higher or lower, or whether the user cannot tell. A higher/lower correction requires the user's approximate amber percentage for the photographed visible sample and may include a short evidence note about focus, lighting, glare, or the counted region. Store the correction separately from the signed AI result and the readiness calculation; it must never silently rewrite either. Bind it to the exact review ID and review-policy version, label both the AI confirmed-to-possible amber range and the user estimate, and state that neither value is a whole-plant percentage. Saving feedback uses no AI credit. Permission to use the linked correction and exact evidence for internal model evaluation or calibration is explicit, off by default, versioned, and independent from saving product-review feedback. Calibration permission also requires the submitter to affirm that they own the photos or have authority to grant this internal use. It does not authorize public display, and an older or incomplete consent record is not an eligible calibration case.

Protected frame extraction also has a 512 MiB source ceiling even when the general video library accepts larger uploads. When reliable local file size is available, reject an oversized source before upload with an exact recovery action; when size metadata is unavailable, the protected server remains authoritative and must fail before analysis dispatch or credit reservation. The protected server samples roughly one low-resolution technical candidate per second, bounded at 600 candidates, and immediately scores decode success, obvious blur, gross exposure/glare, timeline coverage, and near-duplication. Candidate files are temporary: delete every rejected candidate immediately and delete the whole candidate workspace when selection ends. Retain only a quality- and coverage-ranked set that fits the remaining combined 80-image ceiling, the per-frame limit, and the retained-set byte budget. Eighty is a maximum, never a target; a shorter or repetitive video may retain far fewer frames. Keep a compact digest-bound non-image manifest with candidate count, technical rejection counts and reasons, duplicate clusters, timeline buckets, selected timestamps, quality scores, byte totals, and exact retained evidence IDs. A standalone workspace review must restore the newest private source video with only its exact linked generated frame set after navigation or reload; it must not mix frames from an older standalone video or require a Grow. Do not infer trichome biology from discarded pixels or claim that every sampled candidate received provider analysis.

The exact retained frames are private Harvest evidence and must independently satisfy the ordinary macro, site-role, focus, lighting, and glare rules. Preserve selected adjacent pairs when they support comparison of the same gland head or tightly matching region. Mark one frame in each pair as the counting anchor and the other as comparison-only; the comparison frame may inform persistence versus glare but must never add an independent head tally. Diffuse whiteness that persists while focus, camera angle, or the highlight position meaningfully changes may be recorded as lower-confidence `likely_cloudy_persistent`; it is a bounded visual observation, not a chemical measurement, and cannot by itself make a complete distribution usable. Whiteness that blooms, clips, glows, flashes, or moves with the light or viewing angle is `glare_obscured` and must not count as cloudy. Unrelated photos are not a sequence, and sampled frames do not establish continuous motion. A sharp neutral-light still that directly resolves opaque gland-head contents may support `direct_cloudy`. Amber may be easier to distinguish, but it still requires persistent coloration in a sharp visible gland head rather than one warm reflection. If selection fails, preserve the private source-video record, use no incomplete or orphan frame as AI evidence, spend no analysis credit, and let the user retry the durable job or add sharp still photos.

Retained frames remain private and unselected for export by default. Never include the source video, rejected candidates, unselected frames, GPS/EXIF, private record or upload IDs, storage URLs, provider identifiers, or receipt secrets, and never turn the action into a feed post, public link, or other publication. A completed securely attested Harvest result may likewise be shared only through a separate explicit action that revalidates the receipt and produces a bounded readable summary without media or technical identifiers. Failed, deleted, incomplete, or unattested results are not shareable. Any future public Harvest copy must use the separately reviewed public-copy lifecycle rather than this private export path.

When a server-attested visible-sample estimate is usable, the calculator and saved run must also retain its five maturity buckets, counted-head total, count source, counting confidence, exact region basis, directly confirmed amber floor, possible amber total, cloudy-versus-glare uncertainty, and the inspected-area-only boundary. Dry/cure combines measured room/container conditions, air dew point, an optional measured surface-to-dew-point margin, airflow, density, duration, aroma, texture and representative interior checks. Room temperature and RH must be simultaneous real readings. Start temperature, RH, airflow and density blank; never replace missing evidence with common targets or plausible defaults. An incomplete measurement set returns `insufficient_evidence`, keeps numeric outputs blank and tells the user what to measure next.

Record days in the current stage and light exposure as explicit evidence. Drying and curing material should remain protected from light, with brief work light distinguished from continuous room light or direct light. Light exposure is a quality-preservation concern supported by `PMID 6643`; it does not determine mold safety. Keep unknown light exposure unknown rather than assuming darkness from a photo or room label.

A 24-hour task is the next measurement/inspection checkpoint, never a finish date. Use 10-14 days as the owner-supplied controlled-drying planning window, with the explicit limitation that material, cultivar, airflow, temperature, RH and handling can shorten or extend it. A hot, fast, low-humidity dry may reach an endpoint in 5-7 days but should be surfaced as a quality-loss or overdry concern, not presented as the preferred target. Longer than 14 days can occur, but it is not recommended as a routine target and must trigger a condition/quality review. No elapsed-time range marks drying complete: require measured trends, representative exterior/interior checks, aroma, texture and an equilibrated container reading. `PMC9404914` supports method-dependent drying variability and the need to avoid a universal clock.

Air temperature minus air dew point describes the saturation margin of the entered air reading; it is not a surface condensation margin. Calculate and label a surface-to-dew-point margin only when the user or a saved sensor record supplies the coldest relevant material, rack, wall, container or package surface temperature. If no surface temperature exists, condensation remains `not_assessed`. During cure, record jar or bag RH only after the reading has equilibrated, together with measurement time, sensor/source and observed duration.

Do not label mold risk as low, safe or ruled out from one room reading, an image or the absence of visible growth. Use cautious concern states, preserve the evidence used, missing information and limitations, and tell the user to inspect representative interior sites when readings, airflow, density, aroma or texture raise concern. Musty/ammonia odor, abnormal texture or visible growth is a stop-and-inspect signal, not an image diagnosis. Photos may document drying structure and visible surface concerns but can never provide temperature/RH values or rule mold in or out.

Dry / Cure Guard is a cannabis/hemp workflow. Require an owned eligible grow at the API boundary before calculating or saving a ToolRun; navigation gating alone is insufficient. Save the measured snapshot, dew point, available surface margin, light condition, stage day, timing limitations, risk signals, sensor/time notes, tasks and final quality outcomes to the linked harvest batch and grow history for run comparison.
