# Authentication and support-email production evidence

Date: 2026-08-15

## Scope and privacy boundary

- Source: connected `support@growpathai.com` Gmail mailbox.
- Method: read-only Gmail search and message metadata review.
- No email was sent, modified, labeled, archived, or deleted.
- Raw tokens, message identifiers, and complete message bodies are deliberately not
  retained in this document.
- Unrelated private uploads were not inspected.

## Retained delivered evidence

The mailbox contains genuine GrowPathAI messages from `noreply@growpathai.com` that
prove delivery for:

- initial account-verification email;
- verification resend to the QA alias;
- password-reset email to the QA alias;
- support bug-report delivery for Personal Home, Personal Forum/Q&A, Personal Course
  creation, and older Facility/Profile/Task/AI routes.

The current mailbox inventory also contains vendor, integration, billing-wallet, and
hat-manufacturing traffic. Those messages are not counted as product-user acceptance.

## Evidence still absent

The read-only inventory found no genuine delivered example for:

- content-report moderation alerts for Forum, Feed, product, course, video, or live;
- Facility invitations or assigned-task notifications;
- selected-device notification delivery;
- paid course/product/subscription purchase, enrollment, settlement, cancellation,
  refund, dispute, or gift-claim loops;
- independent GrowPathAI user feedback outside the owner's accounts.

The absence of those messages is recorded as an open acceptance boundary, not treated
as proof that the workflows fail and not replaced with fabricated records.
