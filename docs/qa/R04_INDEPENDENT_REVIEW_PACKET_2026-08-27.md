## Purpose and boundary

This is a short independent usability and product-quality review, not a second implementation
backlog and not a repeat of the completed canonical crawl. The reviewer should identify what a
real person cannot find, understand, trust or finish. Existing accepted behavior stays closed
unless the reviewer supplies a current reproducible defect on the frozen release.

- Use a separate reviewer account. Never share an owner or Platform Admin password.
- Start with the reviewer's own Personal workspace. Add only explicitly approved test-workspace
  access needed for Commercial or Facility review; do not grant Platform Admin.
- Do not charge money, publish content, invite other people, disclose private records, delete an
  account, rotate credentials, contact authorities or mutate retained owner evidence.
- Use clearly labeled review data. Do not represent test products, inventory or regulated goods as
  available for sale.
- Stop and record the exact route/state if a workflow asks for a real provider credential, payment,
  legal authorization, physical-device action or privileged role the reviewer does not own.
- Suggestions are evidence, not automatic requirements. Each finding receives an owner decision
  before implementation. Accepted changes go to an isolated preview and require owner approval
  before production.

## Reviewer missions

The reviewer should work from ordinary visible navigation and search. Direct URLs are supplied
only after recording a discoverability problem.

1. **Personal first-use loop** — understand the home screen, start or inspect a Grow, find AI
   tools, run a no-charge path where available, understand a result, find Saved Runs and reach the
   next useful action.
2. **Plant ID, Grow and Nature separation** — confirm that identifying a plant, starting a Grow
   from a result and publishing a geolocated Nature observation are three explicit choices; verify
   that private/home media is not implied to be public.
3. **Grow history and sharing** — find the grow timeline, understand its zoom/detail behavior and
   distinguish private history, export and revocable public sharing.
4. **Community and creator loop** — find Forum/Q&A, videos, Lives, following, comments, reports and
   share/copy actions; note unclear source, destination, privacy or moderation state.
5. **AI result quality** — assess whether Plant ID, Diagnosis, IPM and Harvest Readiness explain
   evidence, uncertainty, limitations, follow-up views/actions and safety boundaries without
   underselling the tool or inventing certainty.
6. **Commercial workspace** — only if the separate reviewer identity is explicitly given a safe
   Commercial test workspace: inspect brand/profile, storefront, inventory and the eight Business
   Desk tools for discoverability, useful business language and clear draft/review/save/export
   boundaries.
7. **Facility workspace** — only if explicitly invited to a safe test Facility role: inspect
   dashboard, grows/rooms/plants, inventory, tasks/SOPs, AI Tools, integrations, reports and role
   restrictions. A Viewer must not be upgraded merely to make a workflow pass.
8. **Cross-cutting polish** — check Day/Night/Auto, keyboard use where practical, mobile and desktop
   readability, headings, Back behavior, loading/empty/error states, labels, media sizing and
   consistency. Record the actual device/browser and viewport.

## Finding format

Create one row per distinct finding. Do not combine unrelated routes or infer a fix without first
recording the observed problem.

| ID | Date/time and release | Account/workspace/role | Device/browser/viewport | Entry path and exact URL | Action attempted | Actual result | Expected result | Evidence | Severity | Suggested change | Owner disposition | Preview evidence | Production decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R04-001 |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  | pending |  | not approved |

Severity meanings:

- `blocker`: prevents a core task or creates a material security, privacy, billing or safety risk.
- `high`: a major workflow is wrong, hidden or misleading, but another path exists.
- `medium`: meaningful usability, clarity, accessibility or consistency defect.
- `low`: polish or preference with limited task impact.

Owner disposition must be one of `accept`, `reject`, `duplicate`, `already fixed`, `needs evidence`
or `defer with reason/date`. Silence is not approval.
